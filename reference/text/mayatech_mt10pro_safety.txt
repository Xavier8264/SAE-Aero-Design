⚠ Safety Disclaimer – Thrust Stand and Power Analyser

This product is intended for testing electric motors and power systems in a controlled environment.
Please read and follow all safety precautions. Use at your own risk.

Important Safety Warnings

  •   Dangerous moving parts – Spinning propellers can cause serious injury. Always handle
      with extreme care.

  •   Props can shatter – High-speed rotation may cause props to break apart. Do not use
      damaged or unbalanced props.

  •   Secure setup – Firmly attach the thrust stand to a stable, immovable surface before
      operation. An unstable stand is extremely dangerous.

  •   Keep clear of the propeller – Never touch or reach near the spinning prop. Keep body
      parts, loose clothing, tools, and other objects clear at all times.

  •   Power wiring – Ensure all power wires and leads are secured away from the propeller arc.

  •   Personal protection – Always wear safety goggles or face protection when operating the
      device.

  •   Children and bystanders – Keep the area clear of children and untrained individuals.
