# Soda Bottle - 2 Liter

Description
Description

The Soda Bottle (2 Liter) is most common with soft drinks. The bottles are made of plastic or glass with printed labels tightly fitted on their midsection and resealable screw-tops that keep carbonation. Their base may be circular or spot with contour lines. It also has a wider midsection that narrows towards the mouth.

Dimensions & Sizes
Dimensions & Sizes

The Soda Bottle (2 Liter) has an overall height of 12.4” (31.5 cm) and diameter of 4.33” (11 cm). The Soda Bottle (2 Liter) holds a volume of 67.6 oz (2 L).
