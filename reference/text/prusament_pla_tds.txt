                                             Technical datasheet

                                        Prusament PLA by Prusa Polymers

Identification

Trade Name                                                  Prusament PLA/PLA (Blend)

Chemical Name                                               Polylactic Acid

Usage                                                       FDM/FFF 3D printing

Nozzle Temperature [°C]                                     210 ± 10
