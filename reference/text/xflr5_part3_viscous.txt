                                 The transition problem

              The transition from laminar to turbulent flow is a complex problem in 2d and
              even more so in 3d.

  free        Things to note in 2d:
stream air    ●Transition occurs when the amplification factor of spatial waves known as
  flow          Tollmien–Schlichting waves reaches a critical value, i.e. the NCrit factor
              ●Turbulent flow starts with small “sparks” which eventually extend downstream
                to full turbulence

              ●“For low Reynolds number flows, the transition is separation induced”
                (in.T. Cebeci, Modeling and computation of boundary-layer flows, chapter.5.2)
                This is also what Xfoil predicts
