FAQ #157

Student Member Affiliation

Last Updated On
12/13/2024 02:54:17 PM
by

brett.foley

Question

Do members need to be affiliated on both the www.sae.org site and the www.saeaerodesign.com site?

Answer

Only students that need to submit documents and ask rules questions must be affiliated on www.saeaerodesign.com. Otherwise, all team members and faculty advisors must be affiliated on www.sae.org.
