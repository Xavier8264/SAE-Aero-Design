SunnySky X2820 800 KV brushless outrunner -- manufacturer datasheet (clean extract)

Source: SunnySky USA official product page, original black "X Series" X2820 (KV 800 / 920 / 1100)
  https://sunnyskyusa.com/products/sunnysky-x2820-brushless-motors
  Full page archived as library ID sunnysky_x2820_800kv (reference/raw/sunnysky_x2820_800kv.html).
Extracted 2026-09-21 from the page's embedded product description (KV800 tab). Values are copied
as published; the only edits are ASCII units (mOhm, deg C) and table layout.
SunnySky publishes no PDF datasheet for this motor; this page is the datasheet.
Machine-readable test table: reference/data/sunnysky_x2820_800kv_testdata.csv (48 rows, all KV800).

NOT the same product as "SunnySky X Series V3 X2820" (https://sunnyskyusa.com/products/sunnysky-x2820),
which is a different line (blue/white) and has no 800 KV option.

SPECIFICATIONS, KV800
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stator diameter            28 mm
Stator thickness           20 mm
Rotor diameter             35 mm
Shaft diameter             5 mm
No. of stator slots        12
No. of rotor poles         14      (12N14P; 7 pole pairs -> mechanical RPM = eRPM / 7)
Body length (w/o shaft)    42 mm
Body length                61 mm
Motor Kv                   800 rpm/V
Max LiPo cells             3-5S
No-load current            0.9 A at 10 V
ESC                        60 A
Motor resistance           41 mOhm
Recommended prop (in)      APC 13x6.5 / APC 12x6 / APC 12x8 / APC 13x8
Max continuous current     46 A for 30 s
Max continuous power       700 W
Weight                     138 g
Aircraft weight, aerobatic 1400-1800 g (4S, APC 12x6 / 12x8 / 13x6.5 / 13x8)
Aircraft weight, 3D        1100-1400 g (4S, same props)

Other KV versions on the same page, for identification only:
  KV920:  28 mOhm, 1.1 A at 10 V no-load, 52 A / 30 s, 138 g
  KV1100: 19 mOhm, 1.3 A at 10 V no-load, 60 A / 15 s, 138 g

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING NOTES (added by the project, not from SunnySky)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. [VERIFIED by arithmetic] The Watts column is exactly 14.8 V x Amps in every row (e.g. 32.7 x 14.8 =
   483.96). So "Voltage" is the nominal pack voltage and "Watts" is nominal, not a measured V x I.
   The test supply voltage under load is not stated. A fresh real pack will sit above or below
   14.8 V depending on current, so our stand numbers will not match this table exactly.
2. No RPM is published. Kv, Rm and I0 must be measured on our own motor (TEST_PLAN.md Run C).
3. The 9x4.5E, 9x6E and 12x10E props the team owns are NOT in this table. The 12x10E is the heaviest
   load of the five and is the one most likely to exceed 46 A (see TEST_PLAN.md 3.5).
4. The resistance is presumably line-to-line (phase to phase), which is the Rm the Drela first-order
   model uses [INFERRED; SunnySky does not say].
5. Researcher cross-check (2026-09-21, not archived): the manufacturer's China site
   http://en.rcsunnysky.com/x-fixedwingseries/1104.html is reported to list the same 12N14P, 41 mOhm and
   138 g. A reseller (readymaderc.com) lists 140 g. Weigh our motor (TEST_PLAN.md Run A).
