    Description

    Take the guesswork out of getting your power system optimised with the Mayatech MT10 Pro Thrust Stand and Power Analyser. This handy tool lets you check out your motor/propeller combinations easily at home without needing to install anything into a model until you’ve finalised the setup. The stand can handle a wide variety of motors and measure up to 10kg of thrust. High precision linear bearings and thrust sensor give accurate thrust readings down to the gram.

    It is also equipped with a power analyser to measure measure Volts, Amps and Watts. The backlit LCD screen displays the measured parameters in an easy to read format with the thrust value being displayed in a separate screen.

    It has been designed to be as robust as possible. All components including the heavy duty base are made from CNC machined aluminium alloy. Holes are provided in the base to secure it to a table or workbench easily.

    This is one piece of kit that makes you wonder how you ever managed without it!

    Important: Please read Safety Sheet before use.
